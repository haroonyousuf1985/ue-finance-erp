Section 149: Cancelled-Bill Income Threshold Repair

A Cancelled UE/Non-UE Employee bill is now correctly excluded from that person's annual Section 149 threshold going forward - but any bill saved before that fix, while still not yet Paid, may have Income Tax calculated against an inflated threshold because a since-cancelled bill was still being counted. Preview shows exactly what would change without touching anything; Apply Fix then writes the corrected Income Tax/Net Amount to those bills. Only Received/Processed/Passed bills are ever touched - Paid bills are never rewritten. Covers single-entry bills AND bulk-uploaded UE Employee lines (addressed by their own "ParentID-LineNo" ID, e.g. E1042-3) - a Non-UE bulk line still can't be safely auto-corrected (no record survives of which tax method/affidavit status was used per line) and is listed separately below for manual review; use Manual Income Inclusion Override further down, or cancel and re-upload the bulk bill, to fix those.

Section 149: Already-Paid Over/Under-Deductions (Credit / Outstanding)

For a bill that's already Paid, the money has moved - its own Income Tax figure is never rewritten. This is computed cumulatively per employee, not bill-by-bill: every not-yet-reconciled Paid Section 149 transaction of theirs is combined into one holistic total actually withheld vs one total that should have been withheld (given their full, correctly-excluding-cancelled-bills history) - the difference becomes either a credit (owed back to them, automatically knocked off their next Section 149 transaction) or, in the rare mirror-image case, an outstanding amount (still owed by them, automatically collected on their next transaction). No further action is needed after Apply here. Covers single-entry bills AND Paid bulk-uploaded UE Employee lines (addressed by their own "ParentID-LineNo" ID) - a Non-UE bulk line still needs Manual Income Inclusion Override below.

Section 149: Manual Income Inclusion Override

Full manual control over which bills/lines count toward a specific person's Section 149 annual income for a given FY - the direct fallback for anything the tools above can't safely judge on their own, chiefly a bulk Non-UE Employee line. Every taxable bill and bulk line for that person in that FY is listed, across every status, including ones already excluded automatically (Cancelled, Redirected - shown greyed out with the reason, since a manual override on top of those has no effect). Untick "Included" on any item to manually exclude it from that person's threshold calculation from now on - re-tick to bring it back in. This does not rewrite any bill's own Income Tax figure; it only changes what future bills for this person are calculated against.

Section 149: Redirect a Paid Bill to the Correct Beneficiary

Use this when a Section 149 UE/Non-UE Employee bill was Paid to the wrong person by mistake - either a normal single-entry bill, or one beneficiary inside a bulk-uploaded bill (enter that beneficiary's own ID, e.g. E1042-2 for the 2nd line of bulk bill E1042 - only that one beneficiary is affected, every other beneficiary in the same bulk bill is left completely untouched). A bulk Non-UE Employee line isn't supported here (it doesn't retain enough information to redirect safely) - correct one of those manually instead. This does four things together: (1) excludes the original payment, so it's out of every calculation from now on - including the wrong beneficiary's own annual income for future bills; (2) credits the wrong beneficiary with the Income Tax already withheld/deposited under their name on that bill, so it's drawn down automatically on their next real bill; (3) creates a brand-new bill for the correct beneficiary, same Gross Amount, with Income Tax freshly calculated against their own annual threshold - it then goes through Processing/Passing/Payment normally, since the actual transfer to them hasn't happened yet; (4) links both records so either one's Bill Audit View shows the other. This does not reverse the money already sent to the wrong beneficiary's bank account - that recovery, if any, happens outside this system.
