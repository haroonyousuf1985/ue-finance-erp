Answer

Tax Deduction Statement

A complete, auditable justification of every rupee of Income Tax on record for one UE/Non-UE Employee in one FY - every transaction, chronologically, with the FBR section applied, and for a Section 149 slab transaction, exactly which bracket(s) its amount fell into and the rate applied to each portion. Any credit applied, and the running annual income before/after each transaction, are shown too - downloadable as CSV or PDF.
