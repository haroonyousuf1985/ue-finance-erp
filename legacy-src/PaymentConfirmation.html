Generating the IBFT batch only means the file was sent to the bank - it does not mean the money moved. Load the list, tick the few that actually bounced and mark them Failed, then confirm everything else in one go. A bounced bulk beneficiary is separated into its own new bill (e.g. E123-A) and moves to Reconciliation; the rest of the bulk bill is not affected.
